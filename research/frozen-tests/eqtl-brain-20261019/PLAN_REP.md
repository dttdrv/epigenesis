# Third neural-derived set: does a plain k-mer model trained on adult brain predict eQTL direction in sensory neurons? (frozen 2026-10-19, before any label of this set is read)

Why. Direction accuracy of the same adult-brain-trained models in neural-derived cells so far: Aygun progenitors B2 0.581 / M5 0.646, Aygun neurons B2 0.538 / M5 0.600 (n = 80), Jerber differentiating neurons B2 0.600 / M5 0.603, but fetal neocortex B2 0.520. The simple 5-mer logistic (B2) was above chance in iPSC-derived sets and no worse than the Sei-based models there. This asks whether that repeats in a fourth, unseen set.

**Test sets (eQTL Catalogue release 7 SuSiE credible sets, GRCh38).** Primary: Schwartzentruber 2018 iPSC-derived sensory neurons (QTD000514, 98 donors). Lineage control, reported but not gating: Young 2019 iPSC-derived microglia (QTD000559). Single-base variants, **PIP ≥ 0.1** (519 and 272 rows; the 0.5 threshold of earlier plans leaves only 45 and 43), gene on the same chromosome with a GENCODE v39 TSS, label = sign of beta, one row per (variant, gene) with the highest PIP. A lower PIP means some rows are tags of the causal variant, so accuracy is expected to be diluted; the PIP ≥ 0.5 subset is also reported.

**Models.** Exactly M4, M5, B1, B2, B3 of `PLAN.md` trained on the same adult-brain rows, same chromosome rule, grid and cross-validation. No test-set row is used for training.

**Scoring (scored once).** Sign accuracy and AUROC, 5–95% whole-gene bootstrap (2,000 draws, PCG64 20261019). On the sensory-neuron set: R1 lower 5% bound of accuracy(B2) > 0.5; R2 accuracy(B2) > accuracy(B1) and > accuracy(B3) by point value (the simpler alternatives: GC change and the reporter head); R3 lower bound of accuracy(M5) > 0.5 (reported). Label `kmer_eqtl_direction_replicates_in_neural_derived_cells` needs R1 and R2. Reported: the PIP ≥ 0.5 subset; microglia; and a pooled accuracy of B2 and M5 across all neural-derived sets at the common PIP ≥ 0.5 threshold (Aygun progenitor and neuron, Jerber, sensory neuron; one row per variant-gene, first occurrence in that order) as a descriptive summary.

**Limits.** Adaptive: B2 was singled out after seeing it in the first three neural sets (they are not blind for this question). Fine-mapped variants are mostly shared across tissues, so this tests transfer of shared regulatory sequence logic, not new biology. Direction of expression is a molecular readout, not neural structure. Scorer qualified on a synthetic positive and null before scoring; no tuning or re-run after scoring.
