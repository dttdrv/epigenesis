# Zero-shot mechanism model: expressed-TF binding change (frozen 2026-10-19, before any prediction is compared with an outcome)

Question: with **no training on any assay**, does a stated mechanism, "an edit that creates binding sites for transcription factors expressed in the developing cortex raises reporter activity; one that destroys them lowers it", predict allelic effects in neural reporter studies, and does the expression information matter?

**Model Z1.** For each of the 879 JASPAR 2024 CORE vertebrate non-redundant motifs and both strands, take every site that overlaps the edited base in the 150- or 230-base window. Site score s = sum of log2 odds (JASPAR counts + 0.8 pseudocount split over four bases, background 0.25). Site occupancy f = 1/(1 + 2^-(s - s0)) with s0 = 0.8 × the motif's best possible score. Motif effect d = sum over overlapping sites of f(alt) − f(ref). Predicted effect = Σ_motif w · d with w = log2(1 + mean RPKM of the transcription factor) over BrainSpan neocortical samples at 8, 9, 12 and 13 post-conception weeks, set to 0 when the mean RPKM is below 1. Dimer motifs use the mean of the components' RPKM; motifs whose factor name has no BrainSpan gene get w = 0. Motif gain is assumed to activate (single global sign). Nothing is fitted.

**Controls.** U1: same, w = 1 for every motif (no expression information). Znull: Z1 recomputed with the weights permuted across motifs, 200 permutations, PCG64 seed 20261019. Comparator S2 (Siraj-trained k5 + motif head). Ceq7 = z(Z1) + z(S2), fixed equal weights.

**Scoring.** The same held-out variants, families and groups as the earlier frozen six-study tests (family-weighted Pearson r, 2,000-draw whole-family bootstrap, PCG64 20261019, 5–95% intervals, signed utility 50/200). **Primary group: the five studies without gse225815**; all six reported too. Checks: G1 lower 5% bound of r(Z1) > 0; G2 lower bound of r(Z1) − r(S2) > 0; G3 observed r(Z1) above the 95th percentile of the 200 permuted-weight r values (study-equal mean over the five studies); G4 r(Z1) > r(U1). Label `expression_weighted_binding_mechanism_predicts_reporter_effects` needs G1, G3 and G4; beating S2 (G2) is reported separately.

**Limits.** All study outcomes were seen in earlier tests (not blind); no parameter here was chosen from them, and every choice above is fixed before scoring. Motif database, 8–13 pcw window and the global activating sign are single declared choices, not tuned. BrainSpan is bulk tissue, not progenitor cells. Scorer qualified on a synthetic positive and null, then run once. Mac CPU only.
