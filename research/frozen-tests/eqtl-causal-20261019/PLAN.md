# Which natural variant in a fine-mapped block changes expression in developing brain? (frozen 2026-10-19, before any model output)

The earlier eQTL tests asked for the direction of a known causal variant and found nothing reliable. This asks the other half: **which of the variants in one credible set is the one that matters**, from sequence, in cells and tissues of developing brain. Only set sizes were counted before writing this plan (e.g. Walker fetal neocortex: 440 qualifying sets).

**Population.** eQTL Catalogue release 7 SuSiE credible sets, gene-expression datasets, GRCh38. Members are single-base variants; the gene must be on the same chromosome with a GENCODE v39 TSS. A set qualifies when it has 2–30 members, its top-PIP member is a single-base variant with PIP ≥ 0.5, and no other member ties it. Label: the member with the top PIP (one per set). Training: the adult-brain datasets of `eqtl-brain-20261019/PLAN.md`; sets repeated across datasets are collapsed to one per (gene, top variant), keeping the highest PIP. **Primary test:** Walker 2019 fetal neocortex. **Secondary pool (neural-derived cells):** Aygun 2021 progenitors and neurons, Jerber 2021 (all 20 datasets), Schwartzentruber 2018 sensory neurons, Young 2019 microglia, one set per (gene, top variant). Chromosome rule: a test set on chromosome c is scored by a model trained only on sets from other chromosomes.

**Features (never use PIP, beta or p-value).** Strand-aware distance of the variant to the TSS in nine log-spaced bins; absolute alt−ref Sei scores (40 histone-normalised sequence classes plus the 571 neural/brain profiles, 4,096-base context); phyloP100way (hg38) at the variant and its mean over ±10 bases.

**Models (conditional logit: probability that a member is the label = softmax of a linear score within its set; L2 penalty chosen by leave-chromosome-group-out CV on training sets, top-1 accuracy).** B0 random; B1 member nearest the TSS; B2 distance bins only; B3 highest phyloP at the variant; M4 distance + Sei; **M5 distance + Sei + phyloP (primary)**.

**Scoring (scored once).** Top-1 accuracy (ties share credit); pooled within-set AUROC; 5–95% bootstrap over genes (2,000 draws, PCG64 20261019; sets sharing a gene resampled together). Checks on Walker (lower 5% bound > 0): G1 top-1(M5) − top-1(B2) (the strongest simple alternative); G2 top-1(M4) − top-1(B2); G3 top-1(M5) − top-1(B1). Label `sequence_model_prioritises_causal_variants_beyond_distance` needs G1 and a positive point value of top-1(M5) − top-1(B2) on the secondary pool.

**Limits.** PIP comes from statistical fine-mapping, so the label can be a tag of the causal variant; accuracy is bounded by LD. Selection of sets by max PIP ≥ 0.5 is a population definition applied equally to every model. Expression-level, not neural-structure, readout. Scorer qualified on a synthetic positive and null before scoring; no tuning or re-run after scoring. Mac MPS/CPU, no spend.
