# Fixed robust-loss transfer diagnostic

Question: does the fixed Siraj Huber head improve predictions of natural-allele
reporter effects in other neural-cell studies? This is a zero-refit diagnostic
on previously exposed outcomes. It does not replace original Siraj acceptance,
open either confirmation set or establish neural development.

Candidate H is the frozen all-FIT head from siraj-huber-joint-20261019. Primary
comparators JL (its exact squared-loss joint-linear parent) and K5 (the original
FIT-only k5 head) have the same training population. Historical S2 and S3 are
copied from their sealed six-study predictions; they used FIT+TUNING and remain
descriptive controls. No retraining, calibration, rescaling, blend, parameter
search, selected subgroup or post-score nominee switch.

Primary studies are Lee, McAfee, Matoba, GSE221384 and Weiss, following the
existing GSE225815 count-noise correction. All six, including GSE225815, form a
fixed sensitivity analysis. Reuse exactly the existing input-row order, families
and heldout flags. Record all population sizes. The 2-kb coordinate exclusion
is an inherited contract, not a new comprehensive exposure admission. Several
inputs are fetched 150-base genomic windows; Weiss uses the supplied native
ancestral/derived pairs. Faithful feature extraction does not establish that all
windows are identical to the physical assay inserts.

Freeze current source/input/outcome identities before predictions. Inputs and
outcomes are positional legacy exports without outcome IDs; pin both plus their
builder source, require exact heldout ID/family alignment with both sealed prior
prediction archives, and reproduce their per-study S2/S3 correlations after
sealing to verify reuse of the previously scored target/order. This is not a
reconstruction of raw measurements. Do not read replicate halves or infer a noise
ceiling. Targets retain ALT-minus-REF, with derived-minus-ancestral for Weiss.

Recompute raw k5 and all 532 linear log-potential deltas for every heldout native
sequence pair with the original trained feature routines, profile ordering and
scale .003. Compare every motif vector to the first block of the previously
sealed context cache before using it. Apply unchanged coefficients, then seal
all five prediction vectors and lexical magnitude rankings before decoding
outcomes. Preserve the Siraj head/source/review identity chain. Any discrepancy
fails closed; no scoring of an incomplete or mismatched population.

For each study report equal-family Pearson correlation, raw MSE and signed
selection utility at 10, 50 and 200. A panel contains one edit per family copy;
absolute prediction selects it and predicted sign sets direction. Ties use
lexical edit ID. Utility at K is unavailable when the population has fewer than
K families. In particular Matoba has 97 eligible families: U200 is unavailable.
Aggregate each endpoint by equal study weight over its fixed eligible set;
U200 uses the four primary studies other than Matoba. Report the precise study
set for each aggregate. Do not silently shrink a panel.

Use 2,000 paired whole-family-copy multinomial draws, PCG64 seed 20261019, study
order Lee/McAfee/Matoba/GSE225815/GSE221384/Weiss and sorted native family labels.
Report 5th/50th/95th quantiles. Predeclared contrasts are H minus JL, K5, S2 and
S3 for correlation and utility, and comparator MSE minus H MSE. Report every
contrast, plus each study and fixed-five leave-one-study-out point means.
The primary transfer questions are positive mean correlation and positive gains
against same-training JL and K5. Also report stronger historical S2 comparison.
These conditional within-study intervals do not capture adaptive model search,
shared laboratory lineages or uncertainty over a population of laboratories.

Qualify scoring against literal duplicated-family calculations and known
positive/null examples before real scoring. Check input joins, orientation,
full feature reconstruction, source pins, failure/no-overwrite behavior and
seal-before-score. Independently replay saved scores. The original seven Siraj
requirements and their current six-of-seven outcome stay unchanged.

CPU only, one numerical process/thread at a time, no spending/downloads. Bound
prediction and scoring separately to 180 seconds each, retaining attempts and
partial artifacts on failure. No protected-folder edits, commits or pushes.
Siraj RESERVE and Salomon confirmation remain sealed.
