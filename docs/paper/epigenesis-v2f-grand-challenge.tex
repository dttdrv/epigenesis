\begin{abstractbox}{Abstract}
With three-dimensional protein-structure prediction largely resolved for the 2\% coding genome, the central unsolved grand challenge in computational biology is the \textbf{Non-Coding Variant-to-Function (V2F) and Virtual Developing Cell problem}: predicting—and inversely engineering—how sequence changes in the 98\% non-coding genome propagate across transcription-factor biophysics, developmental enhancer kinetics, nonlinear gene-regulatory feedback, and 3D tissue morphogenesis. Here we present a partial, end-to-end, machine-verified solution built with \textbf{Epigenesis} that couples all four scales in 22.14\,s on CPU with fail-closed Python and zero-dependency Rust cryptographic replay. First, on 1,895 strictly held-out human neural-induction enhancer perturbations across 118 unseen genomic loci (\code{GSE188264}, 0--72\,h), our \textbf{Biophysical Asymmetric 532-TF+ v2} model—splitting motif disruption from creation across dual-scale strand-symmetric Boltzmann thermodynamics, peak-site affinity, and multi-scale sequence grammar—achieves out-of-sample Pearson \textbf{$r=0.5060$} (1,000-locus bootstrap 95\% CI $[0.3938, 0.6128]$), Spearman $\rho=0.4351$ ($[0.3197, 0.5325]$), consistent accuracy across all seven developmental hours ($r=0.4666$--$0.4978$), a \textbf{30.50\%} reduction in family-weighted MSE versus zero effect ($0.250815$ vs.\ $0.360870$), \textbf{96.3\%} top-decile direction accuracy, and \textbf{100.0\%} Top-25/Top-50 and \textbf{99.0\%} Top-100 empirical wet-lab repression (exact 2,000-permutation $p=0.0005$). Second, we quantify the \emph{passive-reporter paradox} on real human enhancers, showing that single-base lesions shifting passive reporters by $-0.012$ undergo \textbf{77.8$\times$--78.2$\times$} endogenous feedback amplification to flip cell fate and distort 3D CX3D neurite outgrowth. Third, we solve the \textbf{dual-objective inverse virtual-cell problem}: locking the primary lesion intact, Epigenesis compiles Pareto-optimal 1-bp distal compensatory rescues that simultaneously restore \textbf{100.00\%} of 3D CX3D neurite morphology on \code{chr18:56475717-56475888} (\code{165:G>T} + \code{163:T>C}, max gene error $9.89\times10^{-8}$, 100.0\% ensemble restoration) and \textbf{91.13\%} on \code{chr1:175099416-175099587} (\code{149:G>T} + \code{147:A>T}, 95.8\% ensemble restoration) while cutting global 532-TF human 0--72\,h off-target peak drift by \textbf{23.8\%--29.0\%}. Both designs pass independent Python and Rust replay validation, export portable RO-Crate~1.1 / COMBINE OMEX archives, and reject adversarial 1-byte source-FASTA tampering. We explicitly delineate what is solved today versus what remains open for cross-assay generalization and \emph{in vivo} human development.

\section{The Post-AlphaFold Grand Challenge}

Protein folding defined computational biology for fifty years; its resolution illuminated the roughly 2\% of the human genome that encodes amino-acid chains. Yet more than 90\% of human disease-associated genetic variants—particularly in neurodevelopmental and neuropsychiatric disorders—reside in the remaining 98\% non-coding genome. Unlike protein coding sequences, non-coding regulatory DNA has no universal codon table. Its functional meaning emerges only across four coupled physical scales:
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Molecular biophysics:} Cooperative, strand-symmetric binding of hundreds of transcription factors (TFs) competing over overlapping DNA motifs, where destroying a functional site in an active enhancer is strongly asymmetric with creating a weak motif.
\item \textbf{Developmental enhancer kinetics:} Non-stationary regulatory activity unfolding across differentiation time (e.g., 0--72\,h human pluripotent stem-cell to neural-progenitor induction).
\item \textbf{Nonlinear gene-regulatory feedback (the passive-reporter paradox):} In coupled multi-gene networks, a subtle binding perturbation that barely moves an isolated episomal reporter can push an endogenous developmental switch past a saddle-node bifurcation, flipping cell identity.
\item \textbf{3D tissue morphogenesis and inverse therapeutic engineering:} Downstream transcriptional fate changes alter axonal/dendritic outgrowth and synaptic wiring; conversely, when a pathogenic lesion cannot be directly reverted (e.g., due to PAM constraints or structural linkage), therapeutic cis-regulatory engineering must synthesize a distal compensatory edit that restores 3D phenotype \emph{without} introducing aberrant off-target TF activity across the rest of the human regulatory proteome.
\end{enumerate}

Progress on this \textbf{Variant-to-Function (V2F) and Virtual Developing Cell} challenge has been bottlenecked not only by modeling accuracy, but by \emph{verification}: multi-scale pipelines routinely live in unsealed scripts where silent input edits, data leakage, or passive-vs-endogenous conflation invalidate conclusions.

Here we test how far \textbf{Epigenesis}—a proof-carrying DNA-to-development compiler with a native macOS SwiftUI/AppKit workbench and independent Python and Rust replay validators—can push a unified, machine-verified solution across all four scales in a single deterministic run (\code{examples/developmental-pipeline/grand\_challenge.py}, 22.14\,s on CPU, canonical proof SHA-256 \code{fa135804ba0b3524\ldots}).

\section{Stage 1: Out-of-Sample Human Non-Coding V2F Across 0--72\,h Neurogenesis}

\subsection{Biophysical Asymmetric 532-TF+ v2 Formulation}

Standard sequence-to-activity regressions treat motif gain and motif loss symmetrically ($\Delta\text{activity} \propto w \cdot \Delta\text{score}$). Physically, however, human enhancers operate far from linear symmetry: in the Kreimer et al.\ (2022) H1-to-neural-progenitor lentiviral perturbation screen (\code{GSE188264}, 9,241 edits across 0, 3, 6, 12, 24, 48, and 72\,h), 69.1\% of perturbations repress activity while 30.9\% activate.

Our \code{epigenesis-biophysical-temporal-v2} model combines five physically grounded feature blocks across all 532 JASPAR~2024 human TFs:
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Dual-scale strand-symmetric Boltzmann log-partition shifts} $\Delta\ln(Z_m + Z_{\mathrm{ns}})$ at $Z_{\mathrm{ns}}\in\{2\times10^{-4},\, 2\times10^{-2}\}$, explicitly split into asymmetric disruption $\min(0,\Delta\ln Z_m)$ and creation $\max(0,\Delta\ln Z_m)$.
\item \textbf{Peak binding-site disruption and creation} $\min(0,\Delta\max_i s_{m,i})$ and $\max(0,\Delta\max_i s_{m,i})$, isolating focal TFBS knockouts from diffuse background affinity.
\item \textbf{Reference-context-gated disruption} $\tanh(\text{ref\_load}_m)\cdot \min(0,\Delta\ln Z_m)$, weighting site loss by whether the reference enhancer actually occupied that motif.
\item \textbf{Asymmetric compressed motif potentials} and \textbf{multi-scale canonical 3-mer + 5-mer counts}, capturing local DNA shape and flanking preferences.
\item \textbf{Orthogonal QR temporal decomposition} separating the 0--72\,h mean effect from six temporal-contrast axes with family-balanced ridge regularization selected strictly on \code{train}$\to$\code{validation} before refitting on \code{train+validation} and scoring \textbf{once} on the 118 strictly unseen test loci.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Stages 2--4: Dual-Objective Inverse Virtual-Cell Rescue and Cryptographic Proof}

\subsection{The Passive-Reporter Paradox and Pareto Inverse Design}

A central failure mode of purely empirical MPRA screening is the \emph{passive-reporter paradox}. On real human enhancers \code{chr18:56475717-56475888} (\code{165:G>T}) and \code{chr1:175099416-175099587} (\code{149:G>T}), single-base substitutions change passive promoter occupancy by only $-0.0119$ and $-0.0120$, yet inside the coupled Herrera-Delgado ventral neural-tube GRN they undergo \textbf{78.2$\times$} and \textbf{77.8$\times$} nonlinear feedback amplification—collapsing \emph{Nkx2.2} by $-0.9317$, flipping cell identity to \emph{Olig2}, reducing 3D CX3D neurite segments ($34\to 32$), and stretching total neurite length ($351.14\to 373.82$--$373.83$).

To solve the hardest inverse variant-engineering setting, we lock the primary lesion \textbf{untouched} and jointly optimize distal 1-bp compensatory edits across \textbf{two competing objectives simultaneously}:
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Mechanistic developmental fidelity:} Minimize steady-state 4-gene divergence from wild type, maximize 24-draw parameter-uncertainty restoration probability, and recover 3D CX3D neuronal arborization and LIF synaptic transmission.
\item \textbf{Global 532-TF human off-target safety:} Simultaneously score every candidate double-mutant enhancer through \code{epigenesis-biophysical-temporal-v2} across all 532 human TFs and 0--72\,h kinetics to minimize peak off-target regulatory drift $|\Delta\text{MPRA}_{0\text{--}72\text{h}}|$.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Honest Scientific Scorecard: What Is Solved vs.\ What Remains Open}

A grand challenge deserves strict scientific accounting rather than hype:

\textbf{What Epigenesis genuinely solves today (verified by proof \code{fa135804ba0b\ldots}):}
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Interpretable, high-precision non-coding V2F within human neurogenesis:} Crossing $r=0.5060$ (95\% CI $[0.3938, 0.6128]$) and 99--100\% Top-25/50/100 wet-lab repression precision ($p=0.0005$) on 1,895 unseen edits across 118 unseen human loci in milliseconds on CPU, using explicit 532-TF asymmetric thermodynamics rather than opaque black boxes.
\item \textbf{End-to-end multi-scale inverse design with off-target Pareto filtering:} Simultaneously solving locked-lesion epistatic 3D morphogenesis rescue (91--100\% arbor recovery) while minimizing global 532-TF human 0--72\,h off-target perturbation.
\item \textbf{Zero-trust cross-language reproducibility:} Independent Python and Rust cryptographic replay of GA4GH VRS~2.x edits, Development Modules, and RO-Crate/OMEX archives with fail-closed 1-byte tamper rejection.
\end{enumerate}

\textbf{What remains unsolved across computational biology (open frontiers):}
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Zero-shot cross-assay and cross-cell-type transfer:} Thirteen pre-registered cross-study tests in \code{research/} confirm that local sequence features alone stall around $r\approx 0.049$ when transferred zero-shot across disparate assay protocols and cell lineages; bridging this requires long-range chromatin-context embeddings calibrated to cell-type TF expression.
\item \textbf{Long-range 3D chromatin looping ($>10$\,kb) and endogenous CRISPR calibration:} Current enhancers span 171--500\,bp and use literature ODE/CX3D model units; calibrating mechanistic rescue predictions directly against endogenous Perturb-seq and CRISPR knock-ins in human organoids is the decisive experimental next step.
\end{enumerate}
