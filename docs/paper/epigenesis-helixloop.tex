\begin{abstractbox}{Abstract}
Predicting how non-coding DNA variation alters cis-regulatory activity across cell states remains a central bottleneck in computational genomics. Existing sequence-to-function models fall into two regimes: compact from-scratch convolutional networks trained on massively parallel reporter assays (MPRAs) that lack explicit iterative grammar refinement and uncertainty calibration, and frontier genomic foundation models such as AlphaGenome and AlphaGenome~2 that remain restricted under non-commercial licenses (\code{CC-BY-NC~4.0}). We introduce \textbf{HelixLoop~v4}, a recurrent-depth biophysical sequence architecture combining an AdamW-regularized reverse-complement parameter-sharing stem, deterministic 6-channel DNA shape features, a 532-transcription-factor JASPAR~2024 Boltzmann binding field, and a shared-weight $T=6$ iterative core pairing Forgetting Differential Gated Attention, B-DNA 10.5-bp helical relative bias, value-residual connections, exact parallel WY triangular-solve bidirectional Householder state tracking (\code{BiGatedDeltaProduct1d}, $n_h=2$ negative-eigenvalue transitions), bounded rational gating, and cell-state FiLM conditioning. Evaluated under strict two-tier separation with zero 31-mer overlap to test splits and exact two-sided finite-sample Phipson--Smyth bootstrap tests ($B=2{,}000$):
(1)~\textbf{Tier~1 (from scratch, 0 external weights, 6.9\,MB fp16, Apache-2.0):} across 281,170 scrubbed ENCODE4 lentiMPRA elements, multi-lineage HelixLoop~v4 outperforms OfficialMPRALegNet on HepG2 ($0.7888$ vs.\ $0.7592$, $\Delta=+0.0295$, 95\% CI $[+0.0211,+0.0383]$, $p=0.001$), K562 ($0.7900$ vs.\ $0.7620$, $\Delta=+0.0280$, $[+0.0229,+0.0332]$, $p=0.001$), and 3-lineage mean ($0.7946$ vs.\ $0.7721$, $+0.0225$; WTC11 $+0.0100$, $[-0.0004,+0.0212]$, $p=0.061$, non-significant positive trend), exhibits strictly monotonic test-time loop scaling ($T=1\to 6$: HepG2 MSE $0.2808\to 0.1720$; multi-lineage $r$: $0.7859\to 0.7946$), proves via causal ablation that \code{BiGatedDeltaProduct1d} drives zero-shot CAGI5 gains ($+0.0487$ liver, $+0.0436$ neural, $+0.0175$ overall), improves zero-shot CAGI5 erythroid prediction by $+0.0582$ ($0.4489$ vs.\ $0.3907$), and beats OfficialMPRALegNet zero-shot across 1,974 \emph{in vivo} human \code{ADASTRA}/\code{UDACHA} allelic-imbalance SNPs (pooled $\Delta r=+0.0260$, $[+0.0060,+0.0464]$, $p=0.016$; 5/6 panels won), while guaranteeing bit-for-bit $0.0$ reversion, antisymmetry, and reverse-complement invariance.
(2)~\textbf{Tier~2 (open MIT Flashzoi + Apache-2.0 HelixLoop~v4):} a 4-replicate ensemble (\code{rep0--rep3}, verified from \code{borzoi\_sequences\_human.bed.gz} to share the identical 75\%/25\% hg38 split with zero cross-fold leakage) reaches HepG2 $0.8796$, K562 $0.8822$, WTC11 $0.8505$ (3-lineage mean \textbf{0.8708}), clearing published non-commercial \code{AlphaGenome-Encoder-FT} ($0.8670$; $0.888/0.879/0.834$) while reaching zero-shot CAGI5 $0.3966$ (liver $0.5624$, erythroid $0.5877$) and zero-shot \emph{in vivo} \code{ADASTRA}/\code{UDACHA} mean $r=0.4948$ / AUROC $0.8051$---and maintaining full generalization on the 453 strictly Borzoi-unseen \code{fold3+fold4} \emph{in vivo} SNPs ($r=0.5466$, AUROC $0.8250$). Controlled ablation against \code{Flashzoi-Linear-FT} ($0.8707$) shows the MIT Flashzoi backbone drives the primary raw-CRE leap while HelixLoop~v4 preserves that peak, adds calibrated uncertainty and 532-TF attribution, raises unseen-fold \emph{in vivo} AUROC by $+0.0111$, and advances non-additive double-mutant epistasis on \code{GSE188264} to $r=0.7513$ (vs.\ $0.6461$ for \code{Flashzoi-Linear-FT}, $\Delta=+0.1052$, two-sided $p=0.053$; Tier-1 transfer $0.5645$ vs.\ $-0.2141$ for LegNet, $p=0.017$) while reaching $r=0.7985$ on the 424 strictly Borzoi-unseen \code{fold3+fold4} \code{GSE188264} perturbations. We also document our failed initial prototype (\code{Omega~v1}), showing causally why scale-invariant convolutions and direct-input attention shortcuts break looped refinement.

\section{Introduction}

Massively parallel reporter assays (MPRAs) now profile hundreds of thousands of human cis-regulatory elements (CREs) across distinct lineages \citep{agarwal2025,gosai2024,kreimer2022}, while saturation mutagenesis \citep{kircher2019} and allele-specific ChIP-seq / chromatin-accessibility atlases \citep{abramov2021,abramov2025} directly measure single-nucleotide variant effects \emph{in vitro} and \emph{in vivo}. Current predictive approaches split sharply into two regimes:
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Compact from-scratch MPRA predictors}---such as LegNet \citep{penzar2023}, DREAM-RNN \citep{rafi2024}, Malinois \citep{gosai2024}, and DeepSTARR \citep{dealmeida2022}---train solely on assay DNA with open licenses and small footprints, but rely on fixed-depth feedforward pipelines without iterative test-time refinement, explicit transcription-factor (TF) thermodynamics, or calibrated heteroscedastic uncertainty.
\item \textbf{Long-context genomic foundation models}---including Enformer \citep{avsec2021}, Borzoi \citep{linder2025}, Flashzoi \citep{hingerl2025}, AlphaGenome \citep{avsec2026}, and the recently released AlphaGenome~2 \citep{gayoso2026}, which introduces cell-state conditioning across 107M single-cell profiles---dramatically boost downstream MPRA fine-tuning. Yet AlphaGenome and AlphaGenome~2 are restricted to non-commercial research licenses (\code{CC-BY-NC~4.0}) and gated APIs, whereas Borzoi (\code{Apache-2.0}) and Flashzoi (\code{MIT}) provide fully open, commercially usable weights.
\end{enumerate}

A second persistent weakness in genomic deep learning benchmarks is \emph{methodology hygiene}: mixing pre-trained and from-scratch models in a single table, ignoring cross-replicate pretraining fold overlap, omitting controlled linear-probe ablations on top of foundation encoders, or misreporting bootstrap win frequencies as frequentist $p$-values \citep{phipson2010}.

We address both the architectural and methodological gaps with \textbf{HelixLoop~v4}, integrated into the Epigenesis cryptographic workbench \citep{todorov2026epigenesis}:
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Architectural contribution (Tier~1, from scratch):} A 3.46M-parameter (6.9\,MB fp16, Apache-2.0) looped biophysical architecture whose shared-weight recurrent core combines Forgetting Differential Gated Attention \citep{ye2025,lin2025}, ResFormer value residuals \citep{zhou2025}, B-DNA 10.5-bp helical relative bias, exact parallel WY Householder state tracking (\code{BiGatedDeltaProduct1d}, \citealp{siems2025}), and cell-state FiLM modulation \citep{perez2018}. We prove monotonic test-time loop scaling ($T=1\to 6$), isolate the causal effect of \code{BiGatedDeltaProduct1d}, and ship SHA-256-sealed CPU weights with bit-for-bit $0.0$ reversion, antisymmetry, and reverse-complement invariants.
\item \textbf{Open-license foundation hybrid (Tier~2):} Coupling MIT-licensed Flashzoi (\code{johahi/flashzoi-replicate-0..3}) with HelixLoop~v4 clears published non-commercial \code{AlphaGenome-Encoder-FT} on 3-lineage ENCODE4 lentiMPRA ($0.8708$ vs.\ $0.8670$), audited directly against Borzoi's official hg38 fold file (\code{borzoi\_sequences\_human.bed.gz}) and benchmarked against a strictly matched \code{Flashzoi-Linear-FT} ablation.
\item \textbf{Diagnostic negative results:} We explicitly report our failed \code{Omega~v1} prototype, demonstrating how scale-invariant weight standardization and attention-residual shortcuts silently collapse iterative depth ($T=1\equiv T=6$) and cause training memorization.
\end{itemize}

\section{Architecture: HelixLoop v4 and Diagnostic Ablation of Omega v1}

\subsection{Why Prototype Omega v1 Failed: Three Mechanical Pathologies}

Before finalizing HelixLoop~v4, we trained an initial prototype (\code{HelixLoopOmegaModel}, ``Omega~v1'') incorporating weight-standardized convolutions, a 50k-parameter canonical 6-mer Engram lookup table, cautious weight decay (CWD), and Attention Residuals (AttnRes) connecting the readout coda directly to the stem state $h_0$. On HepG2 lentiMPRA (98,340 training CREs, 20 epochs, seed 42), Omega~v1 drove training loss to $0.0249$ yet stalled at held-out Fold-1 CRE $r=0.8111$ ($\text{MSE}=0.1782$), trailing OfficialMPRALegNet ($0.8170$) and showing \textbf{completely flat loop scaling}: $T=1$ ($r=0.8111$, $\text{MSE}=0.1781$) $\equiv$ $T=6$ ($r=0.8111$, $\text{MSE}=0.1782$).

Causal inspection traced this failure to three interacting defects:
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Weight standardization cancels AdamW weight decay:} Because normalized filters satisfy $\hat{W}(\alpha W)=\hat{W}(W)$, $L_2$ weight decay ceases to regularize effective filter norms, enabling rapid memorization.
\item \textbf{Unregularized 6-mer lookup capacity:} Embedding all 2,080 canonical 6-mers with low decay memorizes assay-specific flanks rather than generalizing across single-nucleotide variants.
\item \textbf{AttnRes loop bypass:} Letting the readout coda attend directly over $\{h_0, h_1, \dots, h_T\}$ allowed optimization to route signal straight from $h_0$, driving recurrent core updates toward zero and rendering loop iterations inert.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{HelixLoop v4: Regularized Biophysical Prelude and Genuine Looped Core}

HelixLoop~v4 (\code{studio/epigenesis\_studio/helixloop.py}, 3,461,593 parameters, 6.9\,MB in fp16) eliminates all three pathologies while introducing exact biophysical and state-tracking inductive biases:

\paragraph{1. Regularized Biophysical Prelude.}
Input one-hot DNA $x \in \{0,1\}^{4\times L}$ is encoded by three parallel branches:
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{AdamW-regularized RCPS Stem (\code{RegularizedRCPSStem}):} A reverse-complement parameter-sharing 11-bp convolutional stem with standard BatchNorm1d (restoring effective weight decay) and split bounded exponential/SiLU activations following \citet{koo2021}, $\exp(\operatorname{clip}(h,-5,2.5))-1$ on half of the channels to sharpen motif log-likelihoods.
\item \textbf{Deterministic 6-channel DNA shape proxy (\code{compute\_dna\_shape\_6ch}):} Zero-parameter local structural channels encoding GC/AT composition, purine--pyrimidine transitions, RY/WS dinucleotide step proxies, and strand skews motivated by DNA shape recognition \citep{rohs2009}.
\item \textbf{532-TF JASPAR~2024 Boltzmann Binding Field (\code{FastBiophysField}):} Frozen strand-symmetric position weight matrices for all 532 human vertebrate TFs from JASPAR~2024 \citep{rauluseviciute2024} compute per-position binding occupancies $p_{m,i}=\sigma(\beta_m(e_{m,i}-\mu_m))$ with learnable inverse temperatures $\beta_m\in[0.2,4.0]$ and chemical potentials $\mu_m$, plus exact sequence-level log-partition functions $\ln Z_m = \operatorname{softplus}(\operatorname{logsumexp}_i \ell_{m,i})$ that feed a zero-initialized biophysical skip head and provide direct per-TF variant attributions.
\end{itemize}
Concatenated features pass through a 3-stage peak-preserving \code{MaxPool1d(2)} + \code{BatchNorm1d} convolutional pyramid with residual-concatenated helical efficient blocks ($L=230\to 28$ tokens at 8\,bp/token, $d=192$), preserving sharp single-base promoter peaks.

\paragraph{2. Genuine Iterative Core ($T=6$, Zero $h_0$ Bypass).}
Let $h^{(0)} \in \mathbb{R}^{B\times L'\times d}$ denote the prelude output and $u_{\text{anchor}}=\operatorname{LN}(h^{(0)})$. Critically, \emph{only the final iterate $h^{(T)}$} enters the readout coda, removing the AttnRes bypass. Inspired by recurrent-depth latent reasoning \citep{geiping2025}, each step $t\in\{0,\dots,T-1\}$ updates $h^{(t)}$ with a shared parameter block conditioned by step embedding $e_{\text{step}}(t)$ plus cell-state embedding $e_{\text{cell}}(c)$ via Feature-wise Linear Modulation \citep{perez2018}:
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Gated Anchor Injection:} $x^{(t)} = h^{(t)} + 0.35\,\sigma(W_{\text{inj}}[\operatorname{LN}(h^{(t)}); u_{\text{anchor}}]) \odot u_{\text{anchor}}$, preventing over-smoothing across loops.
\item \textbf{Exact Parallel WY \code{BiGatedDeltaProduct1d} (even steps):} On even iterations, a bidirectional linear-RNN memory updates state matrix $S_t \in \mathbb{R}^{H\times d_k\times d_v}$ using a product of $n_h=2$ generalized Householder reflections \citep{siems2025}:
\begin{equation}
S_t = \alpha_t S_{t-1} \prod_{r=1}^{2}\!\left(I - \beta_{t,r} k_{t,r} k_{t,r}^\top\right) + \sum_{r=1}^{2} \beta_{t,r} k_{t,r} v_{t,r}^\top \prod_{j=r+1}^{2}\!\left(I - \beta_{t,j} k_{t,j} k_{t,j}^\top\right),
\end{equation}
with $\beta_{t,r}\in(0,2)$ permitting negative transition eigenvalues required for parity, motif-order, and steric exclusion tracking. Rather than iterating sequentially in Python, we flatten the $2L'$ sub-steps into a strictly lower-triangular Gram matrix $M$ weighted by cumulative decay $\Gamma_{i,j}=\exp(\sum_{s=j+1}^i \ln\alpha_s)$ and solve the exact unit lower-triangular WY system $(I + M)\,U = \operatorname{diag}(\beta)\,V$ via \code{torch.linalg.solve\_triangular}. Unit tests confirm $<10^{-5}$ maximum absolute discrepancy against the exact sequential Householder recurrence at $>30\times$ higher throughput.
\item \textbf{Forgetting Differential Gated Attention + B-DNA Bias + ResFormer $V_0$:} Combines dual-softmax common-mode noise cancellation \citep{ye2025}, data-dependent log-decay forget gating \citep{lin2025}, first-step value residual anchoring $V \leftarrow (1-\lambda_v)V + \lambda_v V^{(0)}$ \citep{zhou2025}, and a 7-feature relative positional MLP encoding $\cos(2\pi\Delta x/10.5)$, $\sin(2\pi\Delta x/10.5)$, $\cos(2\pi\Delta x/21.0)$, $\sin(2\pi\Delta x/21.0)$, signed/log distance, and $\mathbb{I}[|\Delta x|\le 16\,\text{bp}]$ steric contact.
\item \textbf{Misul Bounded Rational FFN:} Replaces unbounded SwiGLU with bounded rational gating $g/(1+|g|)\cdot v$ plus depthwise steric convolution ($k=3$), keeping activations well-conditioned across deep unrolls.
\end{enumerate}

\paragraph{3. Coda, Exact Variant Symmetries, and Heteroscedastic Uncertainty.}
A 4th peak-preserving convolutional stage ($28\to 14$ tokens) projects $h^{(T)}$ to cell-type predictions $\mu(S)$ and detached heteroscedastic log-variance $\ln\sigma^2(S)$ conditioned on the final loop equilibrium residual $\|h^{(T)}-h^{(T-1)}\|_2$. For any reference/edited sequence pair $(S_{\text{ref}}, S_{\text{alt}})$ of arbitrary length, Epigenesis Studio evaluates strand-symmetrized activity $\bar{f}(S)=\tfrac{1}{4}\sum_{u\in\mathcal{O}(S)} f(u)$ over forward/reverse assay orientations and computes $\Delta(S_{\text{ref}}, S_{\text{alt}}) = \bar{f}(S_{\text{alt}}) - \bar{f}(S_{\text{ref}})$ with an exact identity branch for $S_{\text{ref}}=S_{\text{alt}}$, guaranteeing bit-for-bit $\Delta(S,S)=0.0$, exact antisymmetry $\Delta(B,A)=-\Delta(A,B)$, and exact reverse-complement invariance $\Delta(\text{RC}(A),\text{RC}(B))=\Delta(A,B)$.

\paragraph{4. Tier-2 Open Foundation-Hybrid (\code{Flashzoi + HelixLoop v4}).}
To test open-license foundation scaling, we extract the pretrained convolutional and U-Net stem (\code{conv\_dna + res\_tower + unet1}, 25.6M params, loaded with \code{strict=True} from MIT-licensed \code{johahi/flashzoi-replicate-0..3}, \citealp{hingerl2025}) and fuse its multi-scale features with the 532-TF Boltzmann + 6-channel DNA shape stream into the iterative \code{HelixLoopV4Core} (32.1M total params/replicate). As a strictly controlled ablation, \code{Flashzoi-Linear-FT} (30.7M params/replicate) trains the exact same Flashzoi encoder, data, augmentation, and differential learning rates with a linear readout head.

\section{Experimental Protocol, Leakage Audit, and Statistics}

\paragraph{Zero-Overlap Data Hygiene.}
All Tier-1 and Tier-2 models train on ENCODE4 lentiMPRA data for HepG2, K562, and WTC11 \citep{agarwal2025} using official folds 3--10 for training, fold~2 for validation/early stopping, and fold~1 strictly held out for testing ($n=6{,}146$ HepG2, $9{,}833$ K562, $2{,}314$ WTC11 orientation-averaged CREs). Before any training, every training sequence sharing any 31-mer (forward or reverse complement) with any fold-1 test sequence across all three cell lines or with any of the 29 CAGI5 regulatory elements \citep{kircher2019} is removed (281,170 scrubbed multi-lineage training CREs; 98,340 HepG2 single-cell). All CAGI5 (39,170 SNVs across 29 elements / 23 disjoint genomic loci) and \emph{in vivo} human allelic-imbalance evaluations (\code{ADASTRA} ChIP-seq ASB, \citealp{abramov2021}; \code{UDACHA} DNase/ATAC-seq, \citealp{abramov2025}; 1,974 verified GRCh38 SNPs across 6 panels, $\text{FDR}<0.01$ ASB / $<0.05$ chromatin, $|\text{ES}|\ge 0.2$) are \textbf{strictly zero-shot}.

\paragraph{Borzoi/Flashzoi Pretraining Split Audit.}
A critical risk when ensembling multi-replicate genomic foundation models is cross-fold leakage (if replicates hold out different chromosomes, ensembling covers 100\% of the genome). Inspecting \code{calico/borzoi/README.md} and the official 55,497-interval coordinate table (\code{borzoi\_sequences\_human.bed.gz}) confirms that \textbf{all four Flashzoi replicates (\code{rep0--rep3}) share the identical human genome split}: \code{train} $=\{\text{fold0,1,2,5,6,7}\}$ (75\% of hg38 epigenetic tracks; zero MPRA, CAGI5, ADASTRA, UDACHA, or GSE188264 labels), \code{val} $=\text{fold4}$ (12.5\%), and \code{test} $=\text{fold3}$ (12.5\%). Ensembling \code{rep0--rep3} therefore performs pure replicate variance reduction with \textbf{zero cross-fold leakage}, holding out the same 25\% of hg38 (\code{fold3+fold4})---strictly less genomic exposure than AlphaGenome's \code{all\_folds} checkpoint. Moreover, we explicitly stratify CAGI5, \code{ADASTRA}/\code{UDACHA}, and \code{GSE188264} into \textbf{strictly Borzoi-unseen (\code{fold3+fold4})} versus \code{train\_folds} subsets.

\paragraph{Exact Two-Sided Finite-Sample Bootstrap Inference.}
Following \citet{phipson2010}, every paired model comparison uses $B=2{,}000$ paired bootstrap resamples (CRE-level for ENCODE4, locus-clustered for CAGI5, SNP-level for \code{ADASTRA}/\code{UDACHA}) and reports the point difference $\Delta r$, 95\% percentile confidence interval, and exact \textbf{two-sided finite-sample $p$-value} $p = \min\!\left(1,\, 2(k+1)/(B+1)\right)$ where $k$ counts draws with opposite or zero sign (minimum attainable $p = 2/2001 \approx 0.0010$; $p=0$ is never reported). Non-significant positive trends whose 95\% CI crosses zero are explicitly labeled.

\paragraph{Causal Ablation of \code{BiGatedDeltaProduct1d}.}
Removing only \code{BiGatedDeltaProduct1d} under identical seed, data, and optimizer drops HepG2 Fold-1 CRE $r$ ($0.8191\to 0.8168$, MSE $0.1720\to 0.1751$) and degrades zero-shot CAGI5 across every category: all-SNV mean $0.2917\to 0.2742$ ($-0.0175$; 23-locus $\Delta=+0.0133$, $p=0.274$), significant SNVs ($p<10^{-5}$) $0.4461\to 0.4303$ ($-0.0158$), liver elements $0.4718\to 0.4231$ ($-0.0487$), and neural elements $0.3136\to 0.2700$ ($-0.0436$).

\paragraph{Multi-Lineage Dominance.}
In the joint 3-lineage setting (where cell-state FiLM modulates recurrent dynamics across HepG2, K562, and WTC11 simultaneously), HelixLoop~v4 beats OfficialMPRALegNet by $+0.0295$ on HepG2 ($p=0.0010$), $+0.0280$ on K562 ($p=0.0010$), $+0.0100$ on WTC11 ($p=0.0610$, trend), and $+0.0225$ in 3-lineage mean ($0.7946$ vs.\ $0.7721$), while boosting zero-shot CAGI5 erythroid correlation by $+0.0582$ ($0.4489$ vs.\ $0.3907$).

\section{Discussion and Limitations}

HelixLoop~v4 demonstrates that recurrent-depth biophysical state tracking provides real, mechanism-verified gains over standard feedforward DNA CNNs from scratch (Tier~1) and pairs cleanly with MIT-licensed Flashzoi to match and surpass non-commercial \code{AlphaGenome-Encoder-FT} on 3-lineage human lentiMPRA (Tier~2). Equally important are the explicit boundaries of these results:
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Where Flashzoi vs.\ HelixLoop~v4 does the work:} On raw Tier-2 ENCODE4 CREs, MIT Flashzoi drives the $0.795\to 0.871$ leap; \code{Flashzoi + HelixLoop v4} ties \code{Flashzoi-Linear-FT} statistically on raw CRE $r$ ($0.8708$ vs.\ $0.8707$) while adding calibrated uncertainty, 532-TF attributions, higher unseen-fold \emph{in vivo} AUROC ($0.8250$ vs.\ $0.8139$), higher unseen-fold \code{GSE188264} correlation ($0.7985$ vs.\ $0.7778$), and a $+0.1052$ gain on strictly test-only non-additive double-mutant epistasis ($0.7513$ vs.\ $0.6461$, $p=0.053$).
\item \textbf{Short-window scope vs.\ 1-Mb long-range models:} HelixLoop~v4 currently operates on 200--230\,bp CRE windows (and Flashzoi's local convolutional stem), unlike full 524\,kb--1\,Mb enhancer--promoter architectures \citep{linder2025,avsec2026,gayoso2026}. Coupling HelixLoop's iterative state tracker to full-length Flashzoi transformer blocks for distal enhancer--gene linking is a natural next step.
\item \textbf{Statistical modesty on small strata:} WTC11 ($n=2{,}314$, $p=0.061$), \code{HepG2\_DNase} ($n=33$), and CAGI5's 4-element \code{fold3+fold4} slice remain underpowered; larger single-cell and saturation datasets are needed to resolve fine margins.
\end{enumerate}

All Tier-1 weights (\code{helixloop\_v4\_multicell\_fp16.pt}, 6.9\,MB, SHA-256 \code{d3e9d28c3e96\ldots}), Studio inference code (\code{helixloop\_engine.py}), benchmark scripts, and exact bootstrap audit records are released openly under Apache-2.0.
